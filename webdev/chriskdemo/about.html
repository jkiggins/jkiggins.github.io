About Chris

Built on hard work and professionalism.

My name is Chris Kashorek, and I'm the owner of Chris K's Tree Service, based out of Avon, NY. I built this company on hard work, professionalism, and doing the job the right way every time. When you call, you speak directly with me — not an office or a middleman.

Behind me is a skilled, hardworking crew that takes pride in every job we complete. As a team based in Avon, NY, we specialize in tree removal, trimming, and stump grinding, proudly serving Livingston and Monroe counties and surrounding areas. We use professional equipment, follow strict safety standards, and treat every property with care and respect. Our goal is simple — deliver high-quality work and leave your yard clean when the job is done.

If you're looking for reliable, fully insured tree service from a team that stands behind its work, we'd be glad to help.
